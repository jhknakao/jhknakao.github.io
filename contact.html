Interested in Joining the NaSCaRS Group?

My research group is intended for Swarthmore College students. I do not take on students from other institutions.


Send me an email expressing your interest. Please mention your class year (e.g., class of 2029), your major/anticipated major, the computer languages that you are comfortable coding in (or if you have no previous coding experience), and whether you are interested in a short-term project versus a long-term project. I require my long-term research students to first do 1-2 semesters of directed readings to learn fundamental background material necessary for research in my field. Furthermore, I expect my long-term students to spend at least two full academic years working with me.

A few things to keep in mind: (1) I generally recommend that students with a math/science/engineering GPA below 3.0 focus on strengthening their coursework before pursuing research with my group. (2) A strong coding background will make the transition to our group's research much smoother. It is okay if you do not have any prior coding experience, but I expect a willingness to learn how to code. (3) Positions in my research group are limited, so I apologize if I am not taking on new students.
